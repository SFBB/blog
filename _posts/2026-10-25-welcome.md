Welcome to my blog. Yes, it is in English, mainly as a space for me to practice my English writing.

I will share my `devlogs`, `technical deep dives`, `tech trivia`, my adventures with `cooking` and `gardening`, and whatever else that catches my interest.

When I watched House of Cards, one scene that struck a chord with me was when Frank used his father's vintage typewriter to write that famous letter to the President --- which ultimately paved his way to the Oval Office. It struck me how powerful words can be and the act of typing out one's own thoughts is so strange and captivating.

Since I am learning English writing with an LLM, please bear with my occasional rough edges.

Though I don't have much confidence in my writing, I still want my blog to be interesting. And I genuinely aspire to reach a level where I can write my own fiction.

Anyway, hope you enjoy reading!
